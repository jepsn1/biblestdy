# Biblica permission request — Bibelen på hverdagsdansk (BPH)

Submit via https://www.biblica.com/permission-request-form/ (allow ~10 business
days). Fallback/parallel route: email support@api.bible asking them to help
pursue BPH access on our key (they offer this for specific translations).

---

**Product name:** biblestdy (biblestdy.com)

**Requesting:** Non-commercial digital display license for *Bibelen på
hverdagsdansk* (BPH), ideally delivered as Digital Bible Library access linked
to our API.Bible key (key id on request) — the same channel Biblica already
uses for its other translations.

**Commercial status — free, no revenue:**
biblestdy is entirely free. There is no paid tier, no subscription, no ads, no
data sales and no revenue of any kind; nobody profits from Biblica's text.
It is an early pilot with a handful of Danish users. If we ever introduce paid
features, reading the Bible text will stay free and never be paywalled — and
we will come back to Biblica for a commercial license before charging for
anything.

**What the product is:**
biblestdy is a personal Bible-study web app built around the experience of
writing in a paper Bible: readers highlight verse text, write margin notes and
attach longer study notes that can be anchored to several passages. The text is
displayed as a fixed, book-like two-column spread. Annotations are anchored to
word positions in the verse text, so a reader's study compounds over the years
and resurfaces whenever they return to a passage.

**How BPH would be used:**
- Display-only rendering of chapter text in the app's reader, alongside the
  English translations we already license via API.Bible (NIV 2011 et al.).
- Text is fetched on demand and cached at most 24 hours; we keep no permanent
  copy, offer no offline/download, no audio and no derivatives. Users'
  annotations reference word positions and never reproduce the text.
- The copyright notice is shown on every chapter (already implemented).

**Why BPH:** Danish readers are our core audience, and BPH is the modern
Danish translation they know and ask for.
